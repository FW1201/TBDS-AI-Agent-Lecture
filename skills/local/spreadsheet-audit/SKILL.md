# Spreadsheet quality and analysis workflow

1. Record workbook/sheet names, row counts, field names, date ranges, units, and formulas.
2. Check duplicate identifiers, blank values, mixed numeric/text scales, inconsistent labels, stale copies, and formula errors.
3. Before changing data, propose a cleaning rule for each issue and ask the user to confirm rules that affect counts or conclusions.
4. Preserve the raw sheet. Put cleaned data in a separate sheet and log every exclusion, mapping, or correction.
5. State the denominator, population covered, time window, units, and limitations for every statistic.
6. Distinguish descriptive observations from interpretation and recommendation. Do not claim survey responses represent nonrespondents or all families.
7. For equipment, report book/physical differences and items needing inventory. Do not convert shortages into an approved purchase.
8. Save a reviewable workbook and a short method note in output; retain formulas or steps that allow recalculation.
