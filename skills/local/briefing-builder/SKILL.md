# Briefing workflow

1. Read only the reviewed report/workbook identified by the user; record its version and date.
2. Propose an audience, decision needed, and a one-sentence takeaway per slide.
3. Use a decision-first structure: purpose, evidence, limitations, implications, next steps, and items requiring approval.
4. Check every number against its source table, denominator, unit, and reporting period.
5. Mark proposed actions as recommendations, not approved decisions. Flag sensitive or identifying content for removal.
6. Save a slide-by-slide outline with source notes in the requested output folder. Do not send or publish.
